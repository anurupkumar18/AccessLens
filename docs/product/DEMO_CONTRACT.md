# AccessLens demo contract

## Required demonstration

The three-minute demo must show one instructor-selected biology region updating two
student views through the same reviewed semantic event. At least one student route
must make the visual referent's label, role, and relationship available as
structured text or requested audio; the spatial route must expose equivalent
non-immersive controls.

## Required evidence

- explicit browser capture permission and visible capture state;
- automatic match or visible manual correction, plus an unmatched state that does
  not invent content;
- ordered two-student synchronization and reconnect behavior;
- a rehearsed fallback explaining when semantic-event replay is used;
- only approved Access Pack content in the live path; and
- one precise data-boundary statement: the relay receives semantic events, not the
  instructor's screen, student preferences, or diagnosis data.

## Claims allowed only with evidence

- Describe a reviewed pack as reviewed only when the corresponding review artifact
  and reviewer are recorded.
- Describe latency, rehearsal, or reliability only with the linked measured run.
- Describe Bedrock authoring only when the displayed result is visibly a DRAFT and
  instructor approval remains required.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim diagnosis-based personalization, grading, mastery, attention,
emotion, disability inference, automated accessibility/legal compliance, measured
learning outcomes, expert review that has not occurred, or that AR alone serves a
fully blind student.

## Ticket impact

Every delivery ticket names its demo impact. A ticket may be marked `DONE` only
when its demo impact and the evidence it requires are recorded.
