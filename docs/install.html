AccessLens

An instructor shares a slide. Every student follows it in the format they can actually use — focused view, structured text, speech, or a spatial 3D scene.

Download the extension

Installing it

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome or Edge.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Open the side panel from the AccessLens toolbar icon.

It is not on the Chrome Web Store, so load-unpacked is the install route. Firefox and Safari use different extension packaging and are not supported yet.

This is a prototype, not a product. Its teaching content has not been reviewed by a subject-matter expert or an accessibility professional. It makes no claim of measured learning benefit and no claim of legal accessibility compliance.

What it does not do

It does not record sessions, infer disability, estimate attention or mastery, grade anyone, or read your Canvas account. Accessibility preferences stay on the student's own device and are never transmitted. Screen capture requires the instructor to click Start and pick a source through the browser's own chooser — it cannot begin silently.
