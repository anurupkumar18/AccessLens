T-21 is resolved by adding `capture.stopped` as a base-only, instructor-only
semantic lifecycle event. It carries no raw media, content identifier, student
data, or new persisted field. The relay will deliver it and retain it as the
latest connection state, but will not close the temporary session. A later
`session.started` reopens the live moment on the same valid session and next
sequence; `session.ended` remains terminal and immediately closes relay delivery.
