## UnseenLab

AccessLens's Part 3 renderer uses independently adapted lifecycle and accessibility
patterns from the `Primitive3DStage` in UnseenLab, including explicit Three.js
cleanup, reduced-motion handling, a visible WebGL fallback, and a parallel semantic
object surface.
